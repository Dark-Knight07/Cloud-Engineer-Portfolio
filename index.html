[ 01 · ABOUT ]

From ops tickets to cloud architecture

I started my career the way a lot of cloud engineers do — on the ground floor of IT Operations at IBM, resolving tickets, resetting accounts, and learning how large organizations actually run their technology day to day. That's where I picked up the instinct that still drives how I work: understand why a system is built the way it is before you touch it.

Today, as a Cloud Support Engineer at Tru Performance LLP, I support the Covideo client across a genuinely wide surface area — Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and Entra ID, AWS infrastructure, Linux servers, Slack, SharePoint and Atlassian. I handle identity and access management, provisioning and deprovisioning, security and compliance tasks, and I write the internal documentation that keeps all of it repeatable.

I hold a B.Tech in Computer Engineering (2024) and a Diploma in Computer Engineering (2021). What I'm working on now is the next step: moving from supporting cloud systems to building and automating them — as a Cloud Engineer, then DevOps Engineer, with AI-driven automation as a specialty I'm actively growing into.
